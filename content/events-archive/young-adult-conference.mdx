Join us for an exciting Young Adults Conference at Faith Baptist Church in Chelsea, MI, designed for ages 18–30. This two-day event is a great opportunity to step away from the busyness of life, be refreshed through God’s Word, and connect with other believers in your stage of life.

The conference begins Friday at 7:00 PM and continues through Saturday, concluding at 4:30 PM. Throughout the weekend, you’ll enjoy meaningful services, encouraging fellowship, and plenty of fun—including pizza, late-night bowling, a bonfire, games, a coffee bar, and a burger bar.\
\
We are also excited to welcome our special speaker, Evan Scofield, who will be bringing challenging and encouraging messages throughout the conference.

Whether you're looking to grow spiritually, build lasting friendships, or simply be encouraged, this conference is for you, and you don't want to miss it! The cost is $25 per person and includes all activities and meals listed.

We’d love for you to join us for this powerful and refreshing weekend!\
