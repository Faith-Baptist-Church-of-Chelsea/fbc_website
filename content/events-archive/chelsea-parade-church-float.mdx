We need all the help we can get to help distribute gospel tracts and candy at the Chelsea Fair Parade on August 29. We will be lining up on Park St. in downtown Chelsea at 11 A.M., before they close the roads (just look for the flags and canons). If you are unable to walk with the float, we will also have some seats on the float for people to throw candy.\
\
We would like everyone to wear patriotic colors to match our float so if you have anything red, white or blue (or all three) please wear it!\
\
We also need candy! If you are able to donate candy to throw, we would appreciate it! Please do not bring anything on a stick or that will melt (chocolate/taffy.) Please leave all candy in the kitchen downstairs.
