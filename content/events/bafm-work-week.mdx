Join us for our BAFM Work Week as we work together to assemble **7,000 packets** to be given to veterans this Veterans Day! Each packet will include Gospel tracts, thank-you cards, coloring pages, and other encouraging materials.

You can **drop in for a few hours or stay all day**—every hand makes a difference! Lunch and dinner will be provided each day.

On Wednesday evening, our regular service will be held **downstairs**. Depending on our progress, we may continue working on the packets and singing a few hymns instead of having a service.

Come help us show our veterans our appreciation while sharing the Gospel with them!
