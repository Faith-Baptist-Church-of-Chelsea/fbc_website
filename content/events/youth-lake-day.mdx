Every teen is invited to join us on **Saturday, August 22, from 1:00–6:00 PM** at the **Bromley's House** for an afternoon packed with fun, friends, and time on the lake!\
\
We'll be tubing, kayaking, playing games, hanging out, and making some great memories before summer wraps up. Whether you're flying across the water on a tube or just relaxing with friends, you won't want to miss it!\
\
**What to Bring:**

* Swimsuit (**shorts to the knee and a shirt with sleeves are required**)
* Towel
* A dry change of clothes

Invite a friend, and come ready to have a blast! See you there!
