Join us for a special evening of food, fellowship, and Christmas fun at our annual Ladies Christmas Banquet! We'll enjoy a delicious dinner together in the church fellowship hall, followed by a fun gift exchange for both ladies and children.

The cost is just $7 per lady, and girls ages 15 and under are free! Each lady is asked to bring a wrapped gift, new or used, for the adult gift exchange. Children will also have their own gift exchange, so be sure to bring a gift for them, too!

We look forward to a wonderful evening of Christmas fellowship together!
