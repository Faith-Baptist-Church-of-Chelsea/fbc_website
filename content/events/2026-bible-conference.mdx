We're excited to invite you to this year's Bible Conference at Faith Baptist Church! This is one of the highlights of our year, and we can hardly believe it's almost here.

The conference will begin on **Monday, October 12**, with dinner at **5:00 PM** followed by the evening service at **6:30 PM**. The same schedule will continue each night through **Thursday, October 15**. Each service will feature special music and preaching from two guest speakers.

This year's theme is **"Statements of the Cross,"** as we take an in-depth look at each of the seven statements Jesus made while on the cross and the powerful truths they reveal.

We are privileged to welcome our guest preachers this year:

![](/images/content/2026-bible-conference/1.png)  ![](/images/content/2026-bible-conference/2.png)  ![](/images/content/2026-bible-conference/3.png)

### Pastor David Brown | Pastor Tim Crotts | Pastor Tim Fellure

As always, children **12 years old and under** are encouraged to take sermon notes throughout the conference. Any child who completes notes for all **seven sermons** will receive a special prize!

We would love for you to join us for this encouraging week of Christ-centered preaching, Christ-honoring music, and sweet fellowship as we gather around God's Word.
