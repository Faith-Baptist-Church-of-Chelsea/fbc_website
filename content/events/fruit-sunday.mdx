We invite you to join us for our annual Fruit Sunday on Christmas Sunday! This tradition was started by Pastor Jack Story and has continued for many years.\
\
On this special Sunday, each person will be given a bag of fresh fruit to take home and enjoy over the holidays.\
\
Invite a friend to join you at church as we celebrate the birth of our Savior, and we can't wait to see you there!
