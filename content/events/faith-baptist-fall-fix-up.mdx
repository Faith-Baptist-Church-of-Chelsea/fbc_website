Fall is just around the corner, and we’re getting the church ready for the winter! Join us for **Fall Fix Up**, a church work day where everyone is invited to come out and help clean, organize, and care for our church building and grounds.

There will be **jobs for all ages and all abilities**, so there’s something everyone can help with! Whether you can stay all day or only stop by for a short time, **your help is greatly appreciated**.

**Lunch will be provided**, so come ready to work, fellowship, and help make our church property ready for the season ahead!
