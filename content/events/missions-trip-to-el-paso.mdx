If you have never been on a missions trip before, or have been on many, this trip is perfect for you! We will be taking a group from the church down to help distribute scriptures and hold children's services with Bearing Precious Seed El Paso. We will be staying in El Paso, Texas, and then going into Mexico a few times.

If you are interested in going on this missions trip to El Paso, talk to Steven Wireman.

The trip will be from November 6-13, and the cost will be $400, which will cover food, lodging, and ministry while you are there. There will be an additional cost for plane tickets.
