Location: Ella Sharp Golf Course 2800 4th St. Jackson, MI 49203\
Tee Time: 8:00 AM Shotgun Start (everyone starts at the same time at different holes)\
\
Be there at 7:30 to pay $35/person. Pay Lee Shinabery cash or a check to Faith Baptist Church. You can also pay in the church app ahead of time.\
\
Pick who is on your 4-man team, get your cart, then you can practice putting.\
\
If you do not have a full team, we will group you with some other team.\
\
We will have a bottle of water to start and one at the turn. We will have hot dogs, pop, chips, and cookies at the end of the game.\
\
There won't be prizes for the winning team, longest drive, and closest to the hole, but they will be counted.\
\
**Rules\&#xA;**&#x45;verybody have fun!\
We will play a scramble, or best ball.\
There will be no gimmie putts. One of the 4 people need to make the putt.\
Men who are 60 and over, or younger than 16 play from the senior tees.
