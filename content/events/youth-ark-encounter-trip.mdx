# **Youth Trip - Ark Encounter**

Three days in Williamstown Kentucky at the life-size Ark Encounter. If you’re 13 (or 7th grade)–18, you don’t want to miss this one — this is going to be an amazing weekend of fellowship, fun, and seeing the Bible come to life! We have two Airbnbs where we will be staying for the weekend. If you are interested in going on this trip, talk to the Jaworskis for details and to get signed up.\
\
\*Due to space, this trip will only be for the Faith Baptist teenagers and guests will not be permitted.\
\
**Dates:** September 4-6, 2026\
**Drop-off & Pick-up Times:** The bus will be leaving the church Friday afternoon around 5:00 PM and will return sometime late Sunday night.\
**Cost:** $175/teen\
**Location:** Williamstown, KY\
\
