Join us for the **Faith Baptist Youth Group Spaghetti Dinner!** Bring the whole family for an evening of delicious food, fun entertainment presented by our teens, and an exciting dessert auction to wrap up the night. Be sure to bring a little (or a lot) of extra cash to bid on some amazing desserts!

All proceeds will help send our youth on their upcoming trip to the **Ark Encounter** in Kentucky.

Invite your family and friends, purchase your tickets from one of our teens or in the church foyer, and come enjoy a great evening while supporting a wonderful cause. We look forward to seeing you there!
