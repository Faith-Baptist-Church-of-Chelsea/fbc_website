The **TFM 2026 Fall Trimester Practical Skills Class** will focus on the basics of **church audio and video production**. Students will learn the essential skills needed to serve in a church sound booth or media ministry, including how to operate cameras, use a soundboard, understand basic audio and video equipment, and help with common technical issues.

The goal is not just to teach students how to push buttons, but to give them a practical understanding of the equipment and systems they may encounter in a local church. By the end of the class, students should have the knowledge and confidence to **serve in the sound booth, assist with equipment setup, troubleshoot basic problems, and be a helpful part of their church’s audio and video ministry.**
