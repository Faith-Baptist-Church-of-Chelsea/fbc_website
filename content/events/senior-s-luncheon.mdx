All of the senior citizens of our church are warmly invited to join us for our Senior's Luncheon on **October 8 at 11:30 AM**. Enjoy a delicious meal, meaningful fellowship, and a fun afternoon with friends. This luncheon will be a little different than previous ones, as we'll take some time to help BAFM relabel their packets for Veterans Day. We could use all the help we can get, so whether you've been attending for years or are joining us for the first time, we'd love to have you. Feel free to invite a friend to come along—we look forward to sharing this special time together and hope to see you there!
