Join us for our annual **Harvest Sunday!** This special Sunday is a time for our church family to pause and reflect on the goodness of God, giving thanks for all He has done for us and the many blessings He has given.

We will have our main service, followed by a delicious Thanksgiving meal, and everyone is invited to stay and enjoy a time of food, fellowship, and gratitude together. **There will be no Family School or evening service** on this special day.

As we do each year, we will also replace our regular bulletin with our **Praise Bulletin**, filled with testimonies, blessings, and praises from people throughout our church family. We would love for you to share what God has done in your life! **Praise submissions will open November 1.**

Make plans now to join us for this special day of **thanksgiving, fellowship, and praise—and invite a friend to come with you!**
