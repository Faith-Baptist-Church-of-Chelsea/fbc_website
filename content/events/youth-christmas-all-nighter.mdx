Get ready for an exciting night of games, food, and fun at our Youth All Nighter on December 4th & 5th! Teens are invited to join us for an all-night game night packed with activities, laughter, and great fellowship. Be sure to bring a favorite snack or drink to share.\
\
Throughout the evening, we will enjoy some Christmas-themed snacks, movies, and games...game requests are also welcome! We will also have an ugly sweater contest, so be sure to wear the ugliest sweater you can find!\
\
Guests are invited from 7 P.M. to 10 P.M., and then it will be the FBC teens for the rest of the night and morning.\
\
Don’t miss this awesome opportunity to make memories and spend time together with friends!
