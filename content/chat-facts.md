## Visiting

- First-time visitors usually come to the 11:00 AM Sunday morning service; it
  runs about an hour and 15 minutes.
- Park anywhere in the lot; spots closest to the main entrance are for guests.
  Greeters at the door help people find everything.
- Dress: come as you are — some dress up, plenty don't.
- Nobody is singled out: no standing up, no introducing yourself to the room.
- The church is at 4030 Kalmbach Rd, Chelsea MI, just off I-94 Exit 156.

## Services & what they're like

- Sunday 9:45 AM Family School: all ages learn the Bible together in one room
  (taught by Pastor Summers or Pastor Steve). Current study: "Portraits of
  Proverbs" — the characters found in Proverbs. Nursery (0–3) available.
- Sunday 11:00 AM Morning Service, Sunday 6:00 PM Evening Service,
  Wednesday 7:00 PM Midweek Service.
- Preaching is expository — working through the Bible text. The church uses
  the King James Version; visitors may bring any Bible.
- Music: a blend of old hymns and newer spiritual songs; choir most Sundays,
  plus an orchestra. Anyone can join choir or orchestra — no audition; talk to
  Matthew Dowdy, the music director.
- Services stream live on YouTube (@faithbaptistchurchofchelsea) and Facebook.

## Kids & students

- Nursery (ages 0–3) at every service, staffed.
- Sunday 11:00 AM classes: ages 3–4 (Ben & Amanda Bolen), 5–7 (Haley
  Sackmann), 8–12 (Abi Wireman).
- Wednesday 7:00 PM classes: ages 3–7 (Scott & Heather Turnbow), 8–12
  (Moriah Summers).
- Youth group: ages 12–18, Wednesdays 7:00 PM, led by Josiah & Ashley
  Jaworski.
- Check-in uses Planning Center: child gets a name tag, parent gets a matching
  pickup tag. Every class has a teacher plus a helper; additional helpers for
  children with special needs.
- Young adults (18–30): activities organized by Matthew Dowdy, plus the
  Unashamed conference hosted at the church (Instagram:
  @unashamed_fbcconference).
